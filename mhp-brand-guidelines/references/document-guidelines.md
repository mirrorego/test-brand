# MHP Brand Design System — Document-Specific Guidelines

Reference file for generating on-brand code, layouts, and assets. Rules below are constraints, not suggestions — treat "Forbidden" as hard blocks in generated output.

---

## Word / Report Documents
- **Font:** Segoe UI, standard hierarchy
- **Background:** White or Almost White #F6F6F6
- **Primary text color:** Almost Black #262626
- **Accent colors** for headings/highlights: Vital Trust #1612FF or Darkest Blue #00055B
- **Tables:** clear delineation, header row in Vital Trust or a Zircon tone
- No decorative elements outside the MHP visual language

---

## Infographics & Charts
- **Palette:** primarily Vital Trust, Darkest Blue, Brand Grey; Kinetic Kiwi as accent only
- Sprout Mint reserved for green/sustainability themes and positive outcomes
- Icons: MHP icon library only
- Brand elements (slashes, arrows, pills) may be incorporated
- Follow accessibility contrast rules (see colors.md → Approved Accessibility Pairings)
- Avoid overloaded or overly colorful compositions
